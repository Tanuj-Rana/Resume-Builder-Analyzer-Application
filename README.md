🚀 Resume Builder & ATS Analyzer

An AI-powered platform that helps users create professional resumes, evaluate them against Applicant Tracking System (ATS) standards, compare resumes with job descriptions, and receive intelligent suggestions to improve their chances of getting shortlisted.

📖 Problem Statement

Recruiters receive hundreds of resumes for every job posting. Most candidates struggle to create resumes that effectively showcase their skills, achievements, and experience. Additionally, many resumes fail Applicant Tracking System (ATS) screening due to poor formatting, missing keywords, or weak content.

Traditional resume builders primarily focus on design and formatting, whereas job seekers increasingly require intelligent guidance to improve the quality and effectiveness of their resumes.

Our project aims to bridge this gap by combining an intuitive resume builder with AI-powered resume analysis and ATS evaluation.
